Privacy Policy

How I collect and use your information.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to katieportercoaching.com. I'm Katie Porter, a sole-trader life coach, and I'm the person responsible for any personal information collected through this site.

What information I collect

I keep this deliberately minimal. The only information collected through this site is:

  • Name and email address, when you book a free introductory call through Calendly.
  • Email address, if you contact me directly via the email links or address on this site.

I do not use analytics or tracking tools on this site, and I don't collect any information through the Wheel of Life tool beyond what you choose to enter while using it in your own browser.

How I use this information

I use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, arrange and manage your introductory call, and, if we go on to work together, to deliver coaching sessions. I don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

I use Calendly to manage bookings. When you book a call, the name and email you provide are processed by Calendly on my behalf. Calendly may set cookies to support scheduling functionality, such as remembering your time zone. You can read Calendly's own privacy policy at calendly.com/privacy.

Cookies

This site itself does not set its own cookies. The only cookies you may encounter come from the embedded Calendly widget, used purely to support the booking process described above.

How long I keep your information

I keep contact and booking details only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry or deliver coaching sessions, or as required for my own basic business record-keeping.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what information I hold about you, request that it be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Just get in touch using the email address below.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, email me at katieportercoaching@gmail.com.
